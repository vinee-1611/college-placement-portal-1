About the Portal

Mission

To digitize the entire campus placement process of {{ setting('college_name') }} — from student registration and job posting to interviews, selections and offer letters — in a single, secure, and transparent platform.

Key Capabilities
  • Role-based accounts for students, recruiters and placement officers
  • Eligibility-based job applications with CGPA & backlog checks
  • Resume and offer-letter document management (PDF)
  • Interview scheduling across multiple rounds
  • Real-time notifications and announcements
  • Reports, statistics and CSV data export
Technology Stack

Python · Flask · SQLAlchemy · SQLite · HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · Bootstrap 5 · Chart.js · Font Awesome · Flask-Login · Werkzeug
